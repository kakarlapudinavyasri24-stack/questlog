# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment
for everyone contributing to QuestLog.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome feedback and different perspectives.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Help keep discussions productive and on topic.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation from people who violate this code of conduct.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers with
the relevant context. Reports will be handled with care and discretion.
